## Summary

Registers the **`docs` kind word** in the compiled-in harness kind provider — the host-side admission the docs-config contract requires. - `plugin.go` — `NewMeta` adds `{Class:"kind", Word:"docs", InputDef:"#DocsInput"}`; `Invoke` `case "docs"` decodes into `spec.DocsConfig{}`; unknown-word errors remain.
- **NEW in-repo test** `candy/plugin-harness-kind/plugin_test.go` — describe/invoke admission tests that FAIL without the docs registration (RED-proof below).
